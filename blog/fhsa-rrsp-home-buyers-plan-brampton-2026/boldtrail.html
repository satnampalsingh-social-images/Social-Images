THE SHORT ANSWER

Why stacking matters in Brampton

In Brampton, a typical starter home is a townhouse or semi in neighbourhoods like Sandringham-Wellington, Northgate or Bram East. On an insured purchase, the minimum down payment is 5% of the first $500,000 and 10% of the portion above it (for homes under $1.5 million). On an $850,000 home, that works out to $60,000 before closing costs.

Saving that from a regular paycheque takes years. The Tax-Free First Home Savings Account (FHSA) and the RRSP Home Buyers' Plan (HBP) are the two biggest tools the federal government gives first-time buyers, and the CRA confirms you can make qualifying withdrawals from both for the same home, as long as you meet each program's conditions at the time of each withdrawal.

Example: on an $850,000 Brampton home, the minimum down payment is $60,000 ($25,000 on the first $500,000 + $35,000 on the remaining $350,000).

PROGRAM 1

The FHSA in plain English

The FHSA combines the best parts of an RRSP and a TFSA: contributions are generally tax-deductible, and a qualifying withdrawal to buy your first home is tax-free.

  • Room: $8,000 per year, up to a $40,000 lifetime limit.
  • Carry-forward: unused room carries forward, but only up to $8,000. That means you can never contribute more than $16,000 in a single year.
  • Who qualifies: Canadian residents 18 or older (and 71 or younger) who have not lived in a home they or their spouse owned in the current year or the previous four calendar years.
  • Time limit: the account must close by the end of the year of the earliest of the 15th anniversary of opening it, the year you turn 71, or the year after your first qualifying withdrawal.

Tip: room only starts building once you open the account. If you plan to buy in the next few years and haven't opened an FHSA yet, opening one before December 31, 2026 locks in 2026 room even if you deposit later.

PROGRAM 2

The RRSP Home Buyers' Plan in plain English

The HBP lets you take money out of your RRSP to buy a qualifying home without paying tax on the withdrawal — it's essentially a loan to yourself.

  • Limit: up to $60,000 per person.
  • Repayment: 15 years. Normally repayments begin in the second year after the withdrawal, but for first withdrawals made from January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2028, the CRA says repayments are deferred to the fifth year after the withdrawal year. A 2026 withdrawal means your first repayment year is 2031.
  • Watch out: missed repayments are added to your taxable income for that year.

Because the HBP must be paid back and the FHSA doesn't, most buyers use the FHSA first, then top up with the HBP.

BUDGET FOR THE EXTRAS

Don't forget closing costs

Your down payment is only part of the cash you need. In Brampton, plan for Ontario land transfer tax, legal fees, title insurance, a home inspection and adjustments. Since Brampton is outside Toronto, there is no municipal land transfer tax.

Eligible first-time buyers can claim an Ontario land transfer tax refund of up to $4,000, which covers the full tax on the first $368,000 of the price. You must move in as your principal residence within nine months and apply within 18 months of registration. On your tax return, the federal first-time home buyers' tax credit can also be worth up to $1,500.

BEFORE DECEMBER 31

Your year-end checklist

  1. Open your FHSA before December 31 if you don't have one — even a small deposit starts 2026 room.
  2. Top up to $8,000 (or $16,000 with carry-forward) if you can; the deduction can lower your 2026 tax bill.
  3. Check your RRSP balance — funds generally need to be in the RRSP for at least 90 days before an HBP withdrawal to keep the deduction.
  4. Get pre-approved so you know your price range and which programs you'll actually use.
  5. Talk to your lawyer and lender early about withdrawal timing so the money is ready for closing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the FHSA and the RRSP Home Buyers' Plan for the same home?

Yes. The CRA allows qualifying withdrawals from both an FHSA and the HBP for the same qualifying home, as long as you meet the conditions of each program at the time of each withdrawal.

How much can I put in an FHSA in 2026?

Each year adds $8,000 of room, and up to $8,000 of unused room carries forward from the year before, so the most you can contribute in one year is $16,000. The lifetime limit is $40,000.

When do I have to start repaying the Home Buyers' Plan?

For first withdrawals made between January 1, 2026 and December 31, 2028, repayments are deferred to the fifth year after the withdrawal year, and you have 15 years to repay. A 2026 withdrawal means your first repayment year is 2031.

Does this mean I can buy with no down payment?

No. The legal minimum down payment still applies. These programs can let you fund it without using your own regular savings, if you qualify. Closing costs are extra.

Is there a land transfer tax rebate in Brampton?

Brampton has no municipal land transfer tax. Eligible first-time buyers can get an Ontario land transfer tax refund of up to $4,000 if they move in within nine months and apply within 18 months of registration.

Who counts as a first-time buyer for the FHSA?

You generally qualify if you have not lived in a home that you or your spouse or common-law partner owned in the current year or the previous four calendar years.
