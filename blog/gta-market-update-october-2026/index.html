THE SHORT ANSWER

What happened in the GTA housing market in October 2026?

The Toronto Regional Real Estate Board (TRREB) reported 5,603 home sales through its MLS® System in October 2026, down 7.9% from October 2025. New listings came in at 14,173, down 11.9%.

Prices were lower too. The MLS® Home Price Index (HPI) Composite benchmark was down 4.3% year over year, and the average selling price was $1,024,108, down 2.7%.

TRREB said uncertainty around inflation, borrowing costs and the economy kept many GTA buyers on the sidelines.

THE TREND

Is the market turning?

Compared with last year, the numbers are down. But on a seasonally adjusted basis, TRREB reported that sales and new listings were up from September 2026, and both the benchmark and average price edged up month over month.

One month does not make a trend, but it is worth watching. Buyers who wait for a clear bottom often find that competition has already returned by the time it is obvious.

Affordability check: TRREB noted that while monthly mortgage payments on an average-priced home have edged up from a year ago, they are still much more affordable than in 2023 and 2024, because lower prices have helped offset higher borrowing costs.
FOR BUYERS

What this means if you're buying in Brampton or the GTA

Buyers have negotiating power right now. In Brampton neighbourhoods popular with first-time buyers, such as Sandringham-Wellington, Northgate, Bram East and Vales of Castlemore, that can mean room on price, conditions or closing dates.

  1. Get pre-approved first. Know your real budget and rate hold before you shop.
  2. Look at what homes actually sold for, not asking prices, on the streets you like.
  3. Keep your conditions. In a slower market, financing and inspection conditions are more often accepted.
  4. Budget for closing costs on top of your down payment: land transfer tax, legal fees, inspection and adjustments.

FOR SELLERS

What this means if you're selling

With fewer new listings than last year, well-prepared homes still stand out. What matters most in this market:

#1

Price it right from day one

Overpriced homes sit, and longer days on market usually lead to a lower final price.

#2

Presentation

Clean, staged, well-photographed homes get more showings and stronger offers.

#3

Know your local comps

GTA-wide averages hide big differences between neighbourhoods and home types.

Frequently asked questions

Are GTA home prices going down in 2026?

Compared with a year earlier, yes. TRREB reported the October 2026 MLS HPI Composite benchmark was down 4.3% and the average selling price was down 2.7% compared to October 2025. Seasonally adjusted, both edged up from September 2026.

How many homes sold in the GTA in October 2026?

TRREB reported 5,603 home sales through its MLS System in October 2026, down 7.9% from October 2025.

What was the average home price in the GTA in October 2026?

The average selling price was $1,024,108, down 2.7% year over year, according to TRREB.

Is it a good time to buy a home in Brampton?

Buyers currently have more negotiating power than in recent years, but the right time depends on your finances, job security and plans. Get pre-approved, compare recent sold prices and speak with a local agent before deciding.
