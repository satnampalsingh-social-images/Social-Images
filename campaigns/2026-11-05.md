## 2. Research sources and verified facts (verified November 5, 2026)
| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Ontario LTT brackets (single-family residence): 0.5% to $55,000; 1.0% $55,000–$250,000; 1.5% $250,000–$400,000; 2.0% above $400,000; 2.5% above $2,000,000 | Ontario Ministry of Finance, "Calculating land transfer tax", ontario.ca (updated April 22, 2026) |
| Ontario's worked example: LTT on $400,000 = $4,475 | Same page |
| LTT on an $800,000 home = $12,475 (formula on the same page: value × 0.02 − $3,525) | Calculated from the ontario.ca formula |
| First-time homebuyer refund: maximum $4,000 (since January 1, 2017); no LTT on the first $368,000 for qualifying buyers | Ontario Ministry of Finance, "Land transfer tax refunds for first-time homebuyers", ontario.ca (updated February 10, 2026) |
| Requirements: 18+; occupy as principal residence within 9 months; never owned an eligible home anywhere in the world; spouse cannot have owned one while your spouse; Canadian citizen or permanent resident (18 months after registration to become eligible) | Same page |
| Claim at registration, or apply within 18 months of registration | Same page |

Not covered (not verified today): Toronto's separate municipal land transfer tax. Buyers purchasing in the City of Toronto should ask for a separate estimate.
